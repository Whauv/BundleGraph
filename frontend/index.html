How do I identify my laptop port?

USB-C is a small rounded oval. USB-A is the larger rectangular port. A 3.5 mm headphone jack is circular. Check the laptop’s side or its manufacturer support page.

Plain-language terms

XLR is a common locking three-pin connector for microphones. Phantom power is electrical power that a compatible audio interface can supply to microphones that require it. BundleGraph only treats these as compatible when the relevant evidence is available.
