How do I identify my laptop port?

USB-C is a small rounded oval. USB-A is the larger rectangular port. A 3.5 mm headphone jack is circular. Check the laptop’s side or its manufacturer support page.

    Understand the technical checks

    XLR connectors

    A common locking three-pin microphone connection. A specific product is only treated as matching when evidence supports both ends.

    Phantom power

    Electrical power supplied by some interfaces to compatible microphones that require it. Missing support evidence remains unknown.

    Simultaneous microphone inputs

    Two participants using separate XLR microphones generally require two inputs that can be used at the same time.

    Independent monitoring

    Each participant can listen through suitable headphone equipment. The exact requirement comes from the confirmed request.
